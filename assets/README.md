# assets/

Put **your headshot** here as `presenter.jpg` (or `.png`):

```
assets/presenter.jpg
```

- A clean, front-facing photo on a plain background crops best into the circular
  badge. It's centre-cropped to a square automatically.
- It stays on your machine — `presenter.*` is git-ignored, so it's never shared.
- The generated `badge.png` (the circular version) is also git-ignored and rebuilt
  whenever your photo changes.
- No photo here? Videos still render, just without a badge.
